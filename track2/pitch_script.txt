This is GenoBank.io, Track 2 of the Rare Disease, Real Kid MVA hackathon. Research hypothesis. Not medical care.

Track 1 found biallelic BUB1B loss of function. One allele is a stop at leucine 737, in exon 17 of 23. The other is asparagine 1002 to lysine, a kinase missense with AlphaMissense 0.9229. Independent VEP and EVEE both call the stop pathogenic-class. EVEE names Mosaic variegated aneuploidy syndrome 1.

A stop in exon 17 of 23 is nonsense-mediated decay expected. The default remaining protein is the full-length missense allele, not a stable kinase-dead stub. That is closer to the BubR1 hypomorphic mouse than a truncated chain sitting in the cell.

If any truncated chain escapes decay, Cosic family-frequency scoring still maps it. Electron-ion potential at 37.7 residues reads 81 percent of wild type. The N-terminal spindle adaptor is still encoded. Piezoelectric scoring at 88 residues falls to 53 percent. The kinase-scale harmonic is gone. Lysine 668, the SIRT2 site, is on the missense protein for certain.

Three doors. Restore remaining BUBR1 through SIRT2 and NAD plus. Brake extra-mitotic mTOR and AMPK. Do not silence a checkpoint that is already weak.

We do not propose microtubule poisons, MPS1 inhibitors, or aneuploid-cell killers as a constitutional therapy. Baker's BubR1 mice are also the founding senolytic model. Dasatinib plus quercetin is not ranked: no pediatric MVA label, and this child has a rhabdomyosarcoma history.

R is a genotype index, not a probability of benefit. Mechanism, remaining-protein geometry, phenotype, pediatric label, times 1 minus contraindication. Weights were frozen before scoring.

First, nicotinamide, with nicotinamide riboside as the research analog. SIRT2 deacetylates BUBR1 at lysine 668. North 2014: raising NAD plus extends BubR1-hypomorphic mouse median lifespan 58 percent. Responsiveness index 70 of 100. The only ranked candidate whose target is BUBR1 itself.

Second, metformin. An AMPK activator with pediatric use. Tang and Amon used high-dose AICAR as an aneuploid-cell killer. We keep low-dose AMPK as a metabolic buffer, not a cytotoxic selector. Index 53.

Third, sirolimus. BubR1 mice have mTORC1 hyperactivity that tracks muscle wasting. Strongest phenotype match. Drop the rhabdomyosarcoma penalty and sirolimus rises to 71, essentially tied with nicotinamide. With that penalty, the index is 42. That is why it is third, not first.

Rank order is nicotinamide 70, metformin 53, sirolimus 42. A research ranking for a judging panel, not a treatment plan, and not a probability of benefit.

The same remaining-protein map scales to CEP57 and TRIP13 MVA without putting a genome on a laptop. Thank you to the child and family, and to Sage Bionetworks, the MVA Society, Hugging Face, BEACON, AWS, and Anthropic.
