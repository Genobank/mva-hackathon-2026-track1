Brake mTOR / AMPK

Microtubule poisons, MPS1 inhibitors, aneuploid-cell killers, senolytics.

05 · Reject

Do not poison a weak checkpoint. Do not run the BubR1 senolytic playbook here.

Microtubule poisons

Vincristine, paclitaxel: weaken attachment error-correction.

SAC inhibitors

Reversine, alisertib: silence a checkpoint that is already weak.

Aneuploid-cell killers

17-AAG, high-dose AICAR: this genome is mosaic.

Senolytics R 19

Baker 2011/2016 on BubR1H/H. D+Q is not a pediatric MVA label on an RMS background.

Tumour protocols stay tumour protocols. They are not a constitutional MVA therapy.

Only ranked candidate whose target is BUBR1 protein. SIRT2 deacetylates K668. North 2014: SIRT2 extends BubR1-hypomorphic mouse median lifespan 58%.

High-dose nicotinamide can inhibit sirtuins, so C = 0.15. NR is the closer molecule and is not a rare-disease label.
